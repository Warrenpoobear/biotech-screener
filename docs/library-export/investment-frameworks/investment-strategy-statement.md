# Investment Strategy Statement — Darren Schulz
*Drafted June 25, 2026. A durable reference for the household/family-office capital approach and the professional mandate. This is a strategy/philosophy statement, not a compliance IPS or advice.*

## Purpose & first principles
- **Capital preservation is the first rule.** "What kills you is permanent loss of capital." Size every position and commitment for survivability before return.
- **Separate verified facts from recommendations**, favor reproducible/point-in-time-safe data, and gate any automation behind manual validation.
- The overall approach is a **deliberate barbell**: a concentrated quality-compounder core + a systematic, signal-driven biotech satellite, governed by family-office liquidity and pacing discipline, and anchored professionally in real estate.

---

## Layer 1 — Core equity philosophy: Hohn / TCI quality
- **Pricing power above inflation is the single moat test.** Target businesses that can raise price in real terms indefinitely (~20% margins + ~1% real pricing -> profit growth above revenue, compounding).
- **Concentrate.** A tiny investable universe (~200 names globally); hold ~15; **multi-year holds (~8 years average).**
- **Sell on conviction loss, not valuation.**
- **No shorting** (funding risk too high even when right).
- **"AI eats software" thesis (June 2026):** AI agents dissolving per-seat SaaS lock-in re-rate the SaaS model. Filter: can the company still price above inflation once the workflow moat erodes from above? If the moat is workflow lock-in, AI breaks it; if it's genuine pricing power, AI strengthens it. Expression: trimmed Microsoft hard, rotated toward pricing-power compounders (Alphabet as funded destination; GE Aerospace, Visa, Moody's, S&P Global as the reference book). Holds the nuance that this is a real quality-investor disagreement (Ackman took the other side on MSFT) and stress-tests the same "moat isn't the model, it's context" logic against the a16z/Town case.

### Structural exclusions (core book)
Banks, autos, retail, insurance, asset managers, tobacco, airlines, wireless telecom, media, advertising, fossil-fuel utilities.

## Layer 2 — Biotech coinvest satellite: systematic, institutional-signal-driven
- **Coattail-with-discipline:** weight toward names where elite specialists are accumulating, rather than independent stock-picking.
- Built on a **55-manager 13F registry** (49 elite_core + 6 conditional). **Tier 1 conviction: Fairmount Funds, Deep Track Capital, Logos Capital.**
- Fed by **SEC EDGAR monitoring** (13F, Form 4, 13D/G), a **coinvest watchlist (~16 tickers)**, and **PDUFA / clinical-catalyst tracking**; time entries/exits around binary events.
- **Fail-closed on stale or uncertain data** (uncertain/stale data triggers no-action, not a guess).

## Layer 3 — Personal-account expression & tax placement
- The high-turnover, high-variance biotech sleeve is **placed in tax-advantaged accounts** (Roth + Traditional IRAs at Robinhood) — no tax drag on turnover, tax-free upside on speculative names.
- **Funded by rotation, not fresh capital at scale:** trim mega-cap quality/defensives to fund coinvest-basket adds (e.g., the Jun 25, 2026 run sold MSFT/LLY/JNJ/MCK/AVAV/TEVA to buy ALKS, NBIX, ARWR, NTRA, RCUS, SLDB, SYRE, etc.).
- This sleeve is **deliberately distinct in style** from the quality-compounder core — a satellite, not a contradiction. (Full account history: see the Robinhood activity doc.)

## Layer 4 — Family-office / endowment architecture
- **Endowment-style corpus management** with **family-as-dividend-policy spending discipline**: flat-real, smoothing, and Guyton-Klinger guardrail withdrawal strategies; configurable spending-base denominators (total NAV vs spendable resources).
- **7-layer liquidity-modeling stack** across multiple entities (quarterly ledger spine -> entity -> position/account -> cash-flow -> PE pacing -> RE).
- **PE pacing** via Takahashi-Alexander deterministic cash-flow projection (STAIRS market-state adapter), with commitment/recommitment policy and a call-obligation bridge.
- **Strategic + tactical asset allocation:** CMA construction, scenario generation, optimizer selection (MVO / risk-parity / Black-Litterman), regime-conditional evaluation.

## Process & governance (the throughline)
- **PIT-safe, deterministic, reproducible.** Explicit freeze boundaries, pilot sequences, and production locks before anything goes live.
- **AI-augmented research operation** (Town + Hermes) to industrialize monitoring and screening — but automation is gated behind manual validation cycles and operator approval.
- Credentials/context: CFA + CAIA, 30+ years institutional investment management.

## Coherence note
The concentrated quality core and the multi-name, higher-turnover biotech satellite are **philosophically opposite by design**. The barbell holds together only because the biotech sleeve is an intentional, separately-governed satellite/research-tracking expression — not core capital. Confirm this framing whenever the two are evaluated together.

## Suggested review cadence
- Re-confirm thesis and structural exclusions annually, or on a material regime/thesis shift (e.g., the "AI eats software" re-rating).
- Refresh the 13F registry / Tier-1 list and coinvest watchlist on the existing pipeline cadence.
- Reconcile personal-account drift against the satellite mandate after large run days.

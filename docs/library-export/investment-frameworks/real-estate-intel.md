### Governance Caution: Algorithmic Pricing

**The RealPage antitrust case is the cautionary tale.** Key distinction for governance:
- **Third-party revenue management software** sharing competitors' nonpublic data = legal exposure (LivCor $7M settlement explicitly bars this)
- **First-party dynamic pricing** (using only your own rent, occupancy, concession data) = legally separate category

**Governance question for Liv Communities / Westplan:** What is the framework for AI pricing tools before courts fully distinguish the two use cases? Do 55+ communities warrant a different posture given the resident demographic?
